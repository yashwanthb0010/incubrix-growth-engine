## 1. Study Overview & Objective
To rigorously evaluate the empirical effectiveness of the IncuBrix AI Content Engine, a structured preference test was established to measure whether target audience members (digital creators, marketers, agency operators) prefer platform-tailored, hook-driven assets generated by IncuBrix over raw text excerpts.

---

## 2. Participant Recruitment & Screening Criteria
Participants are screened against four qualifying criteria (must satisfy $\ge 2$):
1. **Active Content Creator:** Regularly creates content on LinkedIn, Instagram, YouTube, or Substack.
2. **Operations / Growth Role:** Manages social publishing for a brand, agency, or startup.
3. **Time Investment:** Spends $\ge 5$ hours per week on content production or distribution.
4. **Tool Familiarity:** Currently uses or has evaluated tools like Canva, Opus Clip, Descript, or Buffer.

---

## 3. Informed Consent & Data Privacy Policy
Participants receive the following clear disclosure prior to recording preferences:
> *"Participation is entirely voluntary and anonymous. Responses are collected exclusively to evaluate content format effectiveness for the IncuBrix assessment. No personally identifiable information (PII), email addresses, passwords, or company proprietary data is gathered or stored. Participants may opt out at any time without consequence."*

---

## 4. Test Instrument (5 Evaluated Asset Pairs)
Respondents evaluate 5 paired asset variants:
- **Variant A (Baseline):** Raw, direct paragraph excerpt copied from the source blog without platform restructuring.
- **Variant B (IncuBrix Generated):** Platform-optimized asset with an immediate hook, structured bulleted takeaways, branded typography, verified UTM link, and clear CTA.

### Evaluation Criteria (5-Point Likert Scale):
1. **Hook Strength:** Which variant grabs your attention faster within the first 3 seconds?
2. **Educational Value:** Rate the actionable usefulness of the takeaway (1 = Low, 5 = High).
3. **Save/Bookmark Likelihood:** Would you save this post for future reference? (Yes / Maybe / No)
4. **Click Intent:** Likelihood to click the verified link to explore the full workflow (1 = Unlikely, 5 = Highly Likely).
5. **Format Native Suitability:** Does the asset feel natural to the target platform feed? (1 to 5).
